The DeltaTok flow wall — decoder + conditional variance, not architecture or sampler

The RAE/DiT t-conditioning port (per-block AdaLN-Zero + Gaussian-Fourier t-embed, job 2066610) walls at exactly the same place as the two legacy architectures — three unrelated fixes, one wall to the second decimal, so the network is eliminated. The per-t error profile (eval_deltatok_flow_t_bins.py) then localizes the failure: the model has a ~1% x̂-reconstruction floor near t=1, and the sampler's v = (x̂ − z)/clamp(1−t, 0.05) conversion amplifies that floor 400× in MSE, injecting ~0.12 of structureless variance into every sample. VGGT-World also predicts x̂ — but its loss and its (nonstandard, damped) Euler update never amplify. Same-day outcome: sampler-only tests (Result section) refute amplification as the dominant term — the binding constraint is the frozen decoder's intolerance to latent error, with conditional variance (~0.18) second.

The t-bins probe — where the model is good, and where it is catastrophic

Per-noise-level teacher-forced error (eval_deltatok_flow_t_bins.py, val split, 50 bins, on the _50k baseline whose own sampled MSEToken is 0.2816–0.2919 — same wall family, same space as the curve). Two analytic baselines: the linear (Wiener) optimum for a structureless Gaussian residual, σ²(1−t)²/(σ²t²+(1−t)²) with σ² = 0.160; and the copy baseline x̂ = z_t/t, error ((1−t)/t)².

  • Not latent-blind: error falls 16× from t=0 to t≈1 — kills the "model ignores z_t" and the "cross-cond shortcut" hypotheses in their strong forms.
  • The ~0.010 floor as t→1 is the x̂-parametrization cost: with no input→output skip, the trunk must re-synthesize all 768 tokens even when the answer is in its input. ~1% MSE is its reconstruction fidelity ceiling — roughly invariant to width/depth/conditioning, which is why every architecture walls identically.
  • The v-MSE panel peaks ≈5 at t≈0.95: that is exactly the floor divided by clamp(1−t,0.05)² — the training objective sees the amplification too, but the clamp caps the incentive at 400×, and only ~0.5% of logit-normal(−0.7, 1.4) samples land at t>0.95.

Mechanism — how a 1% floor becomes a 0.29 wall

1 Conversion. pred_mode=x: the sampler needs a velocity and computes v = (x̂ − z)/clamp(1−t, 0.05) (occrae/generation_helper.py:84). Any x̂ error ε becomes ε/(1−t) in v: 2× at t=0.5, 10× at t=0.9, 20× (400× MSE) at t≥0.95.
2 Injection. At t=0.95 the floor (0.011) becomes v-MSE ≈ 4.4 while the true velocity has variance ≈ 1.2 — the velocity signal drowns. Euler errors are correlated step to step (same model, slowly-moving z), so they accumulate rather than average out.
3 Lock-in. Near t=1 the model behaves as a near-copy of its input. Teacher-forced that fixes a 1% error; at sample time its input is mean + accumulated junk, and it copies the junk. Final sample = conditional mean + ~0.12 structureless variance: 0.160 + 0.125 ≈ 0.285 observed.
4 Decode. The frozen DeltaTok decoder was trained on exact latents only; the junk shatters it → LossDepth ~5.9 vs GT-token ceiling 2.44.

Why each earlier fix could not have worked: width, EMA, zero-init, and t-conditioning all improve the model; the wall = σ² (a data constant) + clamp-amplified floor noise (a sampler-formula constant). Whitening and mu-shift reshape the training distribution; they do not touch the amplifier either.

Correction — VGGT-World predicts x̂ too, and how it escapes

The prior journal's recipe table marked our loss "x-pred through v-MSE ≡ v-pred in expectation — exonerated". That compared training objectives, where the equivalence holds. It does not hold at sampling: the parametrizations differ completely in how model error propagates through the Euler update. The differentiator was hiding in the sampler all along.

third_party/VGGT-World/vggt/models/fm.py (their σ = noise level = our 1−t):

  • Training (fm.py:409-433, comment ##xpred): network outputs x̂; loss is ‖(x̂ − x_t)/σ − (x₁ − x_t)/σ‖² = ‖x̂ − x₁‖²/σ² with σ unclamped down to 0.001 → up to 10⁶ weight on near-clean errors (ours clamps at 0.05 → max 400). t is uniform: ~5% of samples land at σ<0.05 vs ~0.5% for our logit-normal(−0.7, 1.4). Their recipe brute-forces the reconstruction floor down exactly where ours tolerates it.
  • Sampling (fm.py:479-480): 
    step_ratio = ((sigma_cur - sigma_next) / (1 - sigma_cur)).clamp(0.0, 1.0)
x = x + step_ratio * (x_hat - x)
    The denominator is (1 − σ) = data-ness, not σ. Consequences:
updatenoisy endclean end
ours (textbook x-pred ODE) z += Δt·(x̂−z)/max(1−t, 0.05) gentle divide by 0.05 → amplifies x̂ error 20×
VGGT-World (damped) z += Δσ·(x̂−z)/(1−σ), ratio clamped ≤ 1 ratio clamps to 1 → jump straight to x̂ (initial noise discarded) ratio ≈ Δσ ≈ 0.02 → error never amplified

Theirs is not a faithful ODE discretization: step 1 replaces the noise with x̂, and every later step gently blends in a fresh x̂ evaluation — deterministic iterative refinement of the conditional prediction, with model error only ever entering scaled by ~Δσ. There is no mechanism to inject amplified junk. Side effect: the sampler is mean-seeking by construction (no sample diversity) — the right behavior for near-deterministic next-frame forecasting, and for our forecast benchmark too.

RAE (SiT-based) genuinely trains velocity — no conversion at either end. Both references therefore share the property that matters: no small-denominator division of model output at sampling.

In-context conditioning — closing the extraction gap job 2096639 (queued)

The regression floor (MSEToken ≈ 0.18) is the sum of two terms: irreducible conditional variance σ² (future ego-motion, dynamic objects, dis-occlusions — genuinely undetermined by frame-0) and an extraction gap — information present in frame-0 that the network fails to convert into accuracy. The current conditioning is the prime suspect for the gap:

  • Static, unprocessed context. Frame-0 enters only as cross-attention kv — raw DA3 layer-12 feats + one parameter-free LayerNorm, identical k/v at block 1 and block 20. Attention can refine what it asks, but no layer ever writes back into the context, so no layer computes over the scene (scene flow, moving-vs-static, geometry propagation must all live inside the delta stream).
  • Per-camera kv. Camera n's K delta slots attend only to camera n's grid; cross-camera structure can only pass through the narrow delta stream's self-attention.
  • Narrow working stream. ~768 delta tokens carry all scene reasoning while ~2–4k tokens of frame-0 state sit frozen — the stream that computes is 3× narrower than the store it reads.

New mode cond_mode=delta_ctx_incontext (VGGT-World / MMDiT-style): the frame-0 patch grid is concatenated into the sequence and processed jointly with the delta tokens by one global self-attention every block — the context is rewritten layer by layer and cross-camera information flows.

seq Layout. [reg | frame-0 ctx (M = N·Hp·Wp) | delta (t·S)], one joint global attention. Frame-0 tokens are clean (timestep 1) and produce no output and no loss (sliced before out_proj); the n_ctx=1 first delta stays clean as before, so loss is on the predicted delta frames only.
AdaLN Per-token. DiT AdaLN is per-frame — a rectangular view(B,t,S,D) broadcast — which can't express the ragged frame-0 block (M ≠ S). So modulation switches to a per-token gather via a group index (context → group 0 @ t=1, delta frame i → group i+1 @ its sampled t). Gated on the mode → every other path is bit-identical.
cost Zero new params. Reuses cross_cond_emb / cross_spatial_pos / camera_embed (shared delta↔context) / temporal_pos (frame-0 = index 0, deltas shifted 1..t); it only drops the per-camera cross-attn blocks. Sequence grows ~4× (adds the M frame-0 tokens) → bsize 16→8.

The run keeps the entire dit recipe (xxlarge_d20, per-block AdaLN, dtok64, whitenpos, n_ctx=1, loss_mode=v, 50 ep) and changes only cond_mode, isolating the conditioning variable against the walled ..._xxl_dit run.

Success signal is MSEToken, not decode. This direction attacks the extraction gap in latent space; decode metrics stay decoder-capped (the Result section's binding constraint, addressed separately by the #1 finetune) — so LossDepth may not move even if extraction improves. Win = the sampled MSEToken (and a 1-step regression readout) falls below the dit run's 0.293 / 0.179. A clear margin ⇒ the extraction gap was real and in-context closed part of it; ≈ the dit run ⇒ the 0.18 is mostly irreducible σ² (then n_ctx=2 / trajectory conditioning are the levers).
