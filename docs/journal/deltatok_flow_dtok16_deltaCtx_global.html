Setup

Flow-matching ViT over K=16 delta tokens/camera from the frozen dtok16 tokenizer (layer-12, current.pth). cond_mode=delta_ctx (first delta token = clean in-sequence context, no cross-attention), attn_mode=global (one attention over all delta tokens), per-camera camera_embed, loss_mode=v (v-MSE, upweights near-clean t→1). Single GPU, config bsize=16 / effective 32 (grad cum 2), cosine LR 1e-4→9.7e-5. Eval samples generate the full trajectory from noise with a 50-step ODE (eval_num_steps=50).

The metrics. LossFlow — teacher-forced velocity MSE at random t along the GT trajectory (the training objective; Train/LossMean is its train-set twin). MSEToken — MSE between the ODE-sampled deltas (integrated from noise) and GT deltas: the honest "did the sample match GT" signal LossFlow cannot see. LossPointmap / LossDepth / LossRaymap — decode the sampled rollout to geometry and compare vs GT on forecast frames. The _tok variants decode the GT deltas through the same decoder — a fixed tokenizer upper bound (flat, frozen tokenizer). Lower is better throughout. Source: occrae/deltatok_flow_trainer.py:34-37,769-800.

Headline

The flow objective converges cleanly and generalizes — but it is a misleading proxy. Train and val LossFlow both fall ~8× and agree to within 0.07, yet the honest sampled metric MSEToken bottoms out at epoch 3 and then drifts up ~15% for the remaining 37 epochs. Under the 50-step ODE rollout, depth recovers (−45 to −55%) but pointmap and raymap degrade (+20 to +51%) and sit 7–15× above the frozen-tokenizer bound. The camera-ray geometry, not the tokenizer, is the ceiling.

Findings

  • Train ≈ val on the flow objective — no overfit. Train/LossMean 28.34 → 0.653; val LossFlow 5.93 → 0.731 (KITTI) / 4.49 → 0.661 (nuScenes). The teacher-forced denoising objective is well optimized and generalizes; both keep improving to epoch ~35–37.
  • Sampled fidelity stalls at epoch 3. MSEToken hits 0.570 / 0.532 at epoch 3, then rises to 0.675 / 0.607 — ending at or above its starting value on KITTI. ~37 epochs of flow-loss reduction buy zero sampled improvement: the teacher-forced loss and the free-running ODE rollout decouple.
  • Depth recovers, pointmap & raymap diverge. Under the 50-step rollout LossDepth improves -55% / -45% (to 3.0× / 2.0× the bound), but LossPointmap (+39% / +27%) and LossRaymap (+51% / +20%) get worse. Radial depth survives sampling; the ray directions drift, dragging up both raymap and the ray×depth pointmap.
  • The ceiling is generative, not the tokenizer. The _tok bounds are flat and low (pointmap 2.74/4.11, raymap 1.79/1.86); the sampler reaches only ~7–15× that. The tokenizer can represent these frames — the bottleneck is generating faithful geometry tokens, especially rays.
Practical read. Select on MSEToken/rollout-geometry, not flow loss — they disagree by design. MSEToken says early-stop ≈ epoch 3. The raymap/pointmap divergence is the prime suspect for the known degenerate-ray / pose failure (cf. the raymapoff sibling run and the "guard degenerate-ray pose est" fix): the ray channel is where sampling breaks down.

Training health

LR cosine 1e-4 → 9.7e-5. GradNorm peaked ~686 in the first steps (warmup), then settled to ~1.8 for the rest of the run — no late-training blow-up on this finite dtok16 checkpoint (unlike the all-NaN dtok32, see deltatok_flow_dtok32_smoketest.html).
