One change: the net predicts velocity instead of the clean token. This drops the 1/(1−t) blow-up the x-arm carries at the clean-context slot — the reason it was tried. But on the generation-quality geometry metrics (sampled rollout of the forecast frames, decoded and scored vs ground truth, lower = better), the v-arm is worse at the full 100k budget on raymap and pointmap and a tie on depth. Worse, the gap widens: the x-arm keeps improving while the v-arm stalls after ~72k.

The setup · one knob

Identical run, twice — only pred_mode flips x → v

x-arm · baseline

pred_mode=x, loss_mode=v

Net outputs the clean token x̂; velocity is recovered as (x̂−z_t)/(1−t). The v-MSE loss then carries a 1/(1−t)² weight that blows up at the clean-context slot (t→1).

v-arm · the twin

pred_mode=v, loss_mode=v

Net outputs velocity directly, so no 1/(1−t) division. Hypothesis: removing that blow-up should stabilise training and improve generated geometry.

Everything else is held fixed. Same frozen tokenizer (compose 1.0, dtok64, epoch_40), same data (waymo consec, 5 frames, cam0, forecast 3–4), same xxl DiT (width 1536, ~1.2B, AdaLN-Zero), SIGReg 0.005, tc128, maxgap9, cond_mode=delta_ctx_cross, 2 clean context deltas.

Verdict

Keep pred_mode=x; v-prediction is a regression here

1
Worse on every geometry metric. At the full 100k budget, v-pred is +29% raymap, +18% pointmap, ≈tie depth. The gap widens from +10% at 72k — x-pred keeps improving as the v-arm stalls.
2
Worse on the flow loss too. v-pred is +3.9% on the teacher-forced objective at 100k — this is not a sampling artifact; the model fits the training target less well.
3
The blow-up was a feature. The 1/(1−t)² weight up-weights the high-SNR slot the forecast leans on. Removing it traded the pathology for a worse prior.
Caveats & not yet checked
  • Both arms now reach ~100k. v-pred endpoint 7.32 / 9.98 / 3.92 (101k) vs x-pred 5.81 / 8.58 / 3.82 (100k); the headline compares at the matched 100k step.
  • Only the compose-1.0 waymo arm. No test of v-pred on the plain tokenizer, alldata, or other tc.
  • Curves are noisy. Raymap / pointmap bounce ±1–2 between adjacent evals; the ranking is read from the trend, and it is consistent.
  • Alternatives untried. logit-normal t, cosine schedule, and a t-independent loss are sibling arms that target the same t→1 issue.
