Key Findings

Read the two arms separately. max_gap 1–4 ran at num_timesteps=5, max_gap 6/8/9 at num_timesteps=10 on a different base config, so loss is not comparable across the boundary. Runs also stopped at different epochs (20 to 80), and 1/2/3 were cancelled rather than converged. max_gap 5 and 7 were never trained.

The two tables average differently. The loss table is the master rank's running mean printed to the log; the pointmap and spread tables are the batch-weighted all-rank mean from TB. Same quantity, up to ~1% apart — max_gap 1 KITTI LossRecon reads 0.1374 in one and 0.1361 in the other.
  • max_gap 1 and 3 collapsed. ZPartRank falls to ~1.7–2.0 — the latent uses barely two directions — while magnitudes blow up three decades (ZRowMeanSquare ~5–10×105 vs ~102 elsewhere). This is the mechanism behind their stuck eval loss (~0.115), not slow convergence.
  • The collapse is not monotone in max_gap. 1 and 3 collapsed; 2 and 4, on either side of them, did not (rank 62–71 and 30–33). At num_timesteps=5 the outcome looks bimodal — an instability that max_gap influences but does not cleanly order.
  • Every healthy run keeps ZMeanAbsMax near 400–730, versus 26k–38k for the collapsed pair. The metric separates the two regimes far more sharply than it varies within either.
  • Within the num_timesteps=10 arm the spread ordering is 6 > 9 > 8 for both rank and total variance — and again not monotone in max_gap.
  • max_gap=9 reaches the best eval loss (0.0128) on the same 49-epoch budget as 6 (0.0176) and 8 (0.0156), while sitting mid-pack on spread — so within this arm lower loss does not track higher rank.
  • max_gap=2 was still improving when cancelled at epoch 29 (eval 0.0363) and holds the highest rank of the nt=5 arm; max_gap=4 is the only nt=5 run trained to convergence (eval 0.0148 at epoch 80).
  • Pointmap AR against GT is pinned to the decoder floor. OrigVsGT is 2.402 m (KITTI) and 3.322 m (nuScenes) for every run, since it decodes untouched DA3 tokens. Every non-collapsed run's PredVsGT_AR lands within 1.3% of it (2.372–2.412 m on KITTI). Tokenizer error does not show up in this metric.
  • PredVsOrig_AR is the pointmap number that separates them. Across max_gap 4/6/8/9 on KITTI it spans 0.264 m (gap 9) to 0.358 m (gap 8), a 1.36× range, where PredVsGT_AR moves 1.7%. Cancelling the decoder's own error is what makes the metric readable.
  • The two datasets disagree on the pointmap ordering. By PredVsOrig_AR, KITTI ranks 9 < 4 < 6 < 8 while nuScenes ranks 8 < 9 < 4 < 6, and there 9 and 4 are tied to within 0.001 m. Only KITTI puts max_gap=9 first, so pointmap does not confirm the eval-loss ranking.
  • AR compounding only bites the collapsed runs. For max_gap 4/6/8/9 the AR PredVsOrig is 1.00–1.07× its teacher-forced twin. For max_gap 1 it is 1.95× (8.15 m vs 4.17 m) and for max_gap 3, 1.56× (10.16 m vs 6.52 m) — the same pair that collapsed on z-spread.
