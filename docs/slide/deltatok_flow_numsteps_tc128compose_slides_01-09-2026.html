Why it disagrees

1.215 at 2 steps, 1.328 at 4, back to 1.246 at 20

LossFlow is a v-MSE against a freshly drawn t, not a property of the emitted sample. It is a sanity check on the weights, not a sampler ranking.

Reading it — what this changes, and what it does not

The sampler is not buying anything on this arm

A 1-step forward pass matches or beats a 20-step ODE integration on all five metrics, at 1/20th the sampling cost. That is a statement about this checkpoint, not about flow matching in general.

Takeaway

Evaluate at 1–2 steps, not 20

Switching the eval default from 20 to 2 steps improves LossRaymap 22%, LossPointmap 15% and MSEToken 13%, and cuts eval sampling cost 10×. Nothing about training changes.

Interpretation

The model is regressing, not generating

If integration helped, error would fall with steps. It rises, and the no-integration baseline is already competitive — the network has learned a conditional mean, and the ODE path walks away from it.

Caveats

What this does not establish

  • One seed, one arm. tc128 compose at ep100 only. No repeat, no other width.
  • ODE mode only. The damped sampler was not run in this sweep.
  • Steps > 20 untested here. The trend is measured over 1–20; 50 and 100 were not sampled on this arm.
  • Non-monotone LossFlow. 1.2145 at 2, 1.3280 at 4, 1.2462 at 20 — unexplained, and it weakens any single-number summary.
