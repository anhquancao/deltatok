How does the tokenizer's channel budget affect flow matching?

Three tokenizer widths — tc128, tc768, tc1536 — fed into the same XXL DiT flow model, all trained to 100 epochs. Depth improves monotonically with more channels. Pointmap and raymap do not — tc1536 (no channel squeeze) is the worst.

Takeaway

tc768 is the best all-round tokenizer width for flow matching

Wins or ties on all three metrics. tc128 is competitive on pointmap/raymap but pays a −9% depth penalty. tc1536's reconstruction advantage does not transfer downstream.

Why tc1536 hurts

More latent dims ≠ easier denoising

The uncompressed code has 12× the floats of tc128 but only ~15% effective rank (PR/Cz). The flow model wastes capacity denoising dead dimensions. Depth is simpler — it recovers anyway.
