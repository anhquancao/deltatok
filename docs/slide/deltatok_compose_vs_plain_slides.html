Does compose 1.0 match the plain tokenizer? Not at matched compute.

One change: an added composition loss (weight 1.0) on top of reconstruction + SIGReg. It competes with pure reconstruction, so compose converges slower. It only reaches the plain run's eval loss after ~30% more steps, and bottoms out lower only after 2× the steps — but the plain arm was stopped at half the budget, so a final-vs-final comparison does not exist.

Verdict

No at matched compute; a modest win only if you pay for 2× the steps

1
Slower to converge. The compose loss competes with reconstruction. Compose trails plain at every matched epoch and needs 1.30× the steps just to reach plain's endpoint.
2
Lower floor, at a price. Compose bottoms at 0.0401 vs plain's 0.0417, but only by epoch 99 — 2× the compute plain was given.
3
Not a fair race. Plain was truncated at epoch 49. Its asymptote is unknown, so "compose is better" is unproven.
