What is being compared

Four DeltaTok tokenizer runs at layer 12, bsize 2, differing only in the number of learned delta tokens K per camera per frame transition (compression ≈ P/K, P ≈ 777 patches/cam). K = 1 is the pre-sweep baseline (fixedBug); K = 8/16/32 are the cosine-LR variants (cos1e3_1e5_b95 = cosine LR 1e-3→1e-5, Adam β₂ 0.95).

The two metrics. Both decode the tokenizer output to a pointmap on the predicted (non-context) views and compare against the pointmap decoded from the original DA3 tokens (PredVsOrig). LossPointmap_PredVsOrig uses the one-shot (teacher-forced) decode; …_AR uses the autoregressive rollout. Lower is better; AR is the harder, deployment-time number. Source: occrae/deltatok_trainer.py:1032,1035.

Findings

  • More delta tokens → lower pointmap loss, monotonically. On KITTI PredVsOrig: K=1 → 2.66, K=8 → 0.74, K=16 → 0.47, K=32 → 0.36. The K=1 baseline (~777:1 compression) is ~7× worse than K=32 — the single-token bottleneck really does cap reconstruction fidelity.
  • dtok32 reaches the lowest losses of the sweep — about 25% below K=16. Its numbers are at the last logged eval (step 50018); K=16 and K=8 run to step 56762.
  • AR ≳ single, gap widens with worse models. Autoregressive rollout is consistently a touch higher than the one-shot decode; the gap is small at K=32 (0.359 vs 0.366 nuScenes-AR ≈ +2%) but large at K=1 (KITTI 2.66 → 3.56, +34%).
  • nuScenes < KITTI for pointmap loss across every run.
Practical read. K=16 lands at KITTI 0.47 / nuScenes 0.45; K=32 reaches ~25% lower again (0.36 / 0.34) — the compression rate trades directly against pointmap fidelity.
