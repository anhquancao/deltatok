tc1536: which z blow-ups recover, and why the norm on z was added

Every tc1536 tokenizer arm with z statistics in its log, read for blow-up and recovery. Also the history of the LayerNorm + bottleneck decision that the blow-ups led to.

Answer

  • Three tc1536 no-bottleneck arms blew up and never recovered.
    • 44465440: SIGReg 0.01, pool 8192, warmup 2000, no compose, no noised decode, bsize 4. Diverged and never trained.
    • 45610897: SIGReg 0.06, pool 8192, warmup 2000, compose 1.0, noised decode τ 0.8 detached, bsize 2.
    • 45725881: SIGReg 0.02, pool 8192, warmup 0, compose 1.0, noised decode τ 0.8 detached, bsize 2.
    The last two sit at ZPartRank 1.0 on a tail but still train.
  • Three tc1536 no-bottleneck arms blew up and recovered by ep 4–6.
    • 44587238→44590128: SIGReg 0.005, pool 8192, warmup 2000, compose 1.0, no noised decode, bsize 2. Recovered at ep 4.
    • 44465441: SIGReg 0.02, pool 8192, warmup 2000, no compose, no noised decode, bsize 4. Recovered at ep 5.
    • 45727710: SIGReg 0.02, pool 24576, warmup 0, compose 1.0, noised decode τ 0.8 detached, bsize 2. Recovered at ep 6.
  • Onset does not separate the groups. All three recovered arms blew up at ep 1. The stuck arms blew up at ep 1 (44465440), ep 0 (45610897) and ep 3 (45725881).
  • The direction of the scale does, in the four arms that log the SIGReg statistic. In 45727710 and 44590128 the scale and the statistic fall together. In 45610897 and 45725881 the statistic collapses while the scale stays high or climbs.
  • Why each arm lands on its side is untested. One seed per arm. At pool 8192, warmup 2000, no compose and no noised decode, SIGReg 0.005 / 0.01 / 0.02 give never / stuck / recovered.
  • The forced bottleneck (tc1536, LayerNorm(1536) + square Linear(1536,1536)) never blows up. All three forced arms stall at the ~0.13 copy plateau instead.

1 Why the LayerNorm + bottleneck exists

datecommit / docdecisionevidence at the time
2026-07-248037cbf, analysis/2026-07-24_tc_width_bottleneck_diagnosis.html Affine pre_bottleneck_norm before z_proj_down, at tc768 (a real 1536 → 768 bottleneck) The non-affine LN after the bottleneck amplified the backward gradient ~60×. The pre-norm gave ~15% lower eval and steadier training. The doc: it "fixes gradients, not capacity".
2026-07-26analysis/2026-07-26_sigreg_layernorm_geometry.html Keep it at tc768. Drop the post-bottleneck LN (z_norm=false) A non-affine LN pins ‖z‖ = √768, which SIGReg's N(0, I) target cannot reach. pre_bottleneck_norm already fills upstream DeltaTok's terminal-norm role.

The 60× reason may no longer hold. It assumed a LayerNorm after the bottleneck. z_norm=false removed that norm. This is inferred from the code, not measured.

  • Recovered: the scale and the statistic fall together. 45727710 (SIGReg 0.02, pool 24576) reads 0.218 at RMS 20684, then both fall every epoch. 44590128 (SIGReg 0.005, pool 8192) reads 0.326 at KITTI RMS 5479, then both fall.
  • 45610897 (SIGReg 0.06, pool 8192, warmup 2000): the statistic collapses first. It drops 9× from ep 0 to ep 1 (0.237 → 0.026) while the scale nearly doubles (52770 → 96619). A broad start alone did not make it recover.
  • 45725881 (SIGReg 0.02, pool 8192, warmup 0): the scale climbs after the statistic has settled. The statistic is 0.021 at ep 1 at unit scale. The scale then climbs to 4142 by ep 5 while the statistic keeps falling.
  • Reading, inferred. A falling statistic next to a large scale means SIGReg has fitted the bulk rows. The scale is then carried by a few rows. The 2026-09-13 analysis found exactly that in 45725881: slot k=44, 1.56% of rows, +2000 to +7000 on channel 1109. Past an amplitude of ~100 the SIGReg restoring force no longer points home.
  • A high statistic is not one-sided. 44587238 reads 0.402 at ep 0 with a scale of 0.0028. The statistic flags "far from N(0, I)" in either direction.

4 Patterns (one seed each)

  • tc1536, no bottleneck, compose 1.0, noised decode τ 0.8: pool 8192 got stuck in 2 of 2. 45610897 is SIGReg 0.06 / warmup 2000. 45725881 is SIGReg 0.02 / warmup 0. Pool 24576 at SIGReg 0.02 / warmup 0 (45727710) recovered. Only 45725881 vs 45727710 differ in one knob: the pool.
  • tc1536, no bottleneck, pool 8192, warmup 2000, no compose, no noised decode, bsize 4: SIGReg weight does not order the outcomes. 0.005 (44465439) never blew up. 0.01 (44465440) diverged for good. 0.02 (44465441) recovered by ep 5.
  • Pool candidate, untested. Pool 24576 runs scale 33.0 against 11.7 at pool 8192 (occrae/deltatok_trainer.py:749) and holds 3× the detached rows. At ep 1 the statistic of 45727710 rose 0.182 → 0.218 while that of 45725881 fell 0.163 → 0.021.
  • Without any norm on z, 4 of 7 tc1536 SIGReg arms end with a healthy code. 44465439 never blew up. 44590128, 44465441 and 45727710 recovered. 44465440, 45610897 and 45725881 stayed stuck.

Implications (untested)

  • The default is the stalling setup. configs/deltatok/train_deltatok.yaml:69 sets force_bottleneck: true. All three tc1536 forced arms stalled: 45678654 (SIGReg 0.06, pool 8192, τ 0.8), 45831175 and 45831174 (SIGReg 0.02, pool 24576, no noised decode). A new tc1536 arm that does not pin force_bottleneck=false inherits that.
  • A LayerNorm on z at tc1536 without the square projection is untested. The prior cycle blames the square Linear(1536,1536) for the stall, not the LayerNorm.
  • Read SIGReg next to the scale in the first 5 epochs, not ZPartRank alone. In the four arms of section 3, a scale that holds or climbs while the statistic falls marked the stuck ones.

Caveats and not done

  • One seed per arm. The recovered-vs-stuck split in section 3 rests on four arms.
  • No z dumps during the blow-ups. Per-slot dumps exist only at ep 10/20/30 of 45725881 and ep 30 of 45727710. "Bulk fitted, tail left" is inferred from epoch means.
  • The August no-compose arms print no SIGReg statistic. Section 3 cannot be read for 44465439, 44465440 or 44465441.
  • Batch layout differs. No-compose arms run bsize 4, compose arms bsize 2, both at effective batch 16. The census does not compare Eval across the two for that reason.
  • The forced arms 45831175 / 45831174 are filed under project OccAny in monitor_jobs.json, and their logs are not cached. Their settings and z scale come from the prior cycle.
  • Not covered. Jean Zay has no tc1536 log with z lines. Arms that predate the z statistics are absent. 45608142 failed after 43 min under 45610897's run name and adds no epochs.
