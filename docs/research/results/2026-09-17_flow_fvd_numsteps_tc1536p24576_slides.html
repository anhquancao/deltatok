What it means for QUESTION 10

A main-table FD needs a stated step count

FD rewards steps and the per-sample losses punish them. The same checkpoint scores FD 15.36 or 4.41 depending on the sampler setting alone.

Reading, not tested

Consistent with a 1-step sample near the conditional mean

A mean-like sample is close to each GT on average, so per-sample losses are low. Its spread is too narrow to match the GT distribution, so FD is high. The FD mean and covariance terms were not logged, so this is not confirmed. The earlier step analysis is in analysis/2026-09-01_flow_numsteps_why_more_steps_degrade.md.

  • Per-image, not video. Frames 3 and 4 are pooled into one distribution, despite the FVD key name.
  • Same weights assumed. current.pth says global_epoch 71; the last logged eval is ep 70.
  • FD terms not split. The mean and covariance parts were not logged.
