Cutting SIGReg does not rescue wide bottlenecks — it makes them worse.

The hypothesis: wider target_channels converges worse because SIGReg holds more channels to N(0,I), so the recon regression is a regularizer tax. Test: halve sigreg_weight 0.005 → 0.002 at fixed tc, everything else identical. The knob works — through epoch 20 the raw SIGReg statistic runs 1.5–2.7× higher, so z really is less constrained where it counts. Recon still gets worse at both widths, and the tc512-vs-tc256 gap barely moves.

read · caveats · next

Q2 is answered against the hypothesis. The wide-tc regression is a rank ceiling.

what to conclude
  • Retire the sigreg-tax explanation. Lower weight, worse recon, gap intact — at both widths.
  • Rank is the state variable, width is not. Rank vs recon r = −0.999 here and −0.97 on the six-width sweep; Cz vs rank is −0.10.
  • Do not generalise from tc512. It is a stuck arm — worse than tc768 and tc1024 on rank despite having fewer channels. Why it stalls at ep 4 is open.
what would change the answer
  • Single seed, one weight step. 0.005 → 0.002 only. A much larger cut (or 0) could behave differently — though an earlier no-SIGReg twin measured a ~10× lower participation ratio.
  • Stopped at epoch 43 of 100. The deficit is narrowing — tc256 peaked at +15.8% (ep 15) and is +6.1% now, tc512 +18.6% (ep 6) → +3.2%. A very late crossover is not excluded, but nothing in the rank curves points at one.
  • No tc128 · 0.002 twin, so the A/B exists only at 256 and 512.
next moves
  • Test the prediction, cheaply. Run tc512 at sigreg 0.01. If rank is the state variable, rank goes up and recon improves. That flips the sign of the knob and is the strongest single check left.
  • Attack the rank ceiling directly — whitening or per-channel rescale before the isotropy term — rather than tuning the weight. The measured floor says SIGReg is a strong scale anchor and a weak isotropy driver.
  • These two arms are cancelled — they were answering a question that is already answered. The released walltime is better spent on the 0.01 arm.
