Caveats — what this calibration does not establish

One checkpoint, one noise draw, single-arm errors

Likely underestimates
  • Only frame-sharing correlation is modelled. Slower structure along a drive (highway vs city stretches) is not, so every SE is probably low. Comparing strides stays fair.
  • KITTI is one drive. Its ~2.2% floor is a within-drive error. All 11 odometry sequences are unseen by every training config, so adding them is the way to lower it.
  • Waymo rows below stride 5 are an artifact: the run started at stride 5, so nothing was thinned and the blocks were oversized. They are not shown.
Scope
  • Single-arm SE, not arm differences. Arms share windows, so the SE of a difference should be smaller. Not measured; it needs a second arm on the same windows.
  • One checkpoint, 20 steps, one noise seed. The ctx2fwd8 arm at ep 24. Other step counts are not calibrated.
  • nuScenes uses the fixed val folder. The 8 corrupted scenes of occ3d_nuscenes_processed are regenerated keyframe-only.
