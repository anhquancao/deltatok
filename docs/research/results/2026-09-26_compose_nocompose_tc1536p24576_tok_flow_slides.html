it also swaps the pair sampler
With compose on, each sample is a triplet: 2 hops encoded, plus a decode of their sum. With it off, each sample is 1 pair, and one gap ~U[1, 9] is shared by the whole batch (occrae/deltatok_trainer.py:1164). So the arm also gets half the recon pairs per step.

the answer — impact of compose at tc1536

Here, compose is what made the tokenizer train. Without it the tokenizer is unusable, and so is its flow.

1
Tokenizer: no compose never trains. Eval 0.1208 vs 0.0219 at ep 70 (5.5×), no better than its ep-0 value. The decoder ignores z: σ = 0.82 moves recon +0.1% (slides 3–5).
2
Flow: nothing to learn, then NaN. Its GT-token ceiling is 2–17× worse. It sits on that ceiling from ep 0. It diverged at iter 15,240, and 46484087 was cancelled 2026-09-26 (slides 12–14).
3
Across tc1536 arms, compose looks like insurance, not a recon win. Compose on: 5 of 5 trained. Off: 2 of 4. At tc128 compose costs +3% single-step recon (results/2026-08-25_compose_vs_plain_slides.html).
