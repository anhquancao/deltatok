Caveats — what this comparison does not establish

This is our benchmark, not the paper's; VGGT-World runs far outside its training regime

Protocol

Paper numbers are not comparable

  • They score one frame. KITTI frame 13 or Cityscapes frame 19, depth AbsRel / δ1 only, after per-frame median scaling.
  • Shorter horizon. Their frames are 0.2 s apart and the mid-term target is 3 steps (0.6 s) ahead. Ours are 0.5 s apart, out to 8 steps (4 s).
  • Pose counts here. Pointmap, raymap and Chamfer use the predicted cameras; their eval never scores pose.
  • Metric scale counts here. Our main row needs DA3's scale; theirs is scale-free.
Setup

What else could move the ranking

  • Domain. cityscapes.pt never saw KITTI, nuScenes or Waymo. DeltaTok trained on Waymo train, so Waymo is not zero-shot for it.
  • Stage 2 is inferred. The file stores no config; epoch 6 matches the authors' default checkpoint_6.pt.
  • Best-of-steps favours DeltaTok. It picks the best of 4 step counts per metric.
  • One noise draw per job. Single-set SE is ~1.6–2.9% of the mean at these window counts.
  • No FD. VGGT-World's tokens live in a different space from the DA3 features FD uses.
