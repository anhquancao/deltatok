Caveats

What this does not establish

  • One checkpoint, one noise draw per job. No other arm, no repeat. ep 24 is mid-training.
  • Noise. Single-set SE is ~1.6–2.9% of the mean at these window counts. Step differences are paired on the same windows, so their noise is smaller, but that was not measured.
  • Per-image FD, not video. The 8 forecast frames are pooled into one distribution, despite the FVD key name.
  • Earlier sweep differs. On Waymo val with 3 given and 2 forecast frames, FD kept falling to 20 steps. deck
  • ODE sampler only, steps ≤ 20. No decode visuals.
