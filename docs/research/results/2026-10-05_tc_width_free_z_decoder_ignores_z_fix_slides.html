2 · the decoder ignores z — z-swap probe on epoch_10.pth

So what is different in a stuck run? Its decoder gives the same output whatever z it gets.

Each checkpoint decodes 64 KITTI windows from x_prev with a changed z. Swap: each pair gets the z of another window at the same step. The test does not depend on z's scale.

3 · the fix: a large-gap decode — the gap each decode spans

Compose adds a decode across two hops. A large gap makes copying x_prev a poor guess, so reading z pays.

The triplet path picks 3 of the 10 frames at random and ignores max_gap (occrae/deltatok_trainer.py:1017). Its hops are short. The composed decode from frame t0 to t2 spans both.

Compose vs triplet only: the one difference is the t0 → t2 decode. Compose escaped on 4 of 7 streams, the triplet path on 0 of 4.
August pair sweep: max_gap ≥ 4 escaped 4 of 4 arms, ≤ 3 escaped 1 of 3. One seed per arm.
0.1 needs it: it escapes with compose and stays stuck without (slide 5).
Against "gap alone": the September pair arm 46250879 decoded gaps up to 9 (mean 5) and stayed stuck. One stream, and escape is partly luck.
Open alternative: the sum constraint za + zb itself. The next slide separates the two.

3 · the fix: the gap, not the sum — training.compose_skip

With z encoded straight from (t0, t2), and no sum anywhere, both runs still escape. The gap is what matters.

compose_skip=true replaces za + zb with a z encoded from the (t0, t2) pair. Its single-step recon is better: 0.0252 vs 0.0343 at ep 8 masked, 0.0248 vs 0.0299 at ep 5 at 0.1. It gives up additivity: LossRecon_Comp, the decode of the summed hop codes, stalls near 0.11.
