verdict — and what the plain arm says about the diagnosis

The missing norm was not the cause.

The ep-31 read blamed the control's stuck code on z being unnormalised at tc1536. The plain arm is the counterexample: same missing norm, and it escapes on its own.

1
The flag works, mechanically. 4.725 M params built, ZRowMeanSquare 1.13 from the first eval, ZPartRank never 1.0. The plan's ep-4 gate passes and the ep-31 diagnosis of what was broken is confirmed by repair.
2
It is not enough, and it is expensive. ×3.2 token recon, ×4.3 round trip, ×6.6 on nuScenes pointmap at matched ep 22 — and the rank lands at 2.3% of Cz, not the plain arm's 15.7%. Scale fixed, rank not.
3
The plain tc1536 also sits at rank 1.0 for epochs 1–3 and escapes at ep 4 with no norm on z, sigreg 0.005 and warmup 2000. So the missing norm cannot be what traps the control. What the control has and the plain arm does not is 12× the sigreg weight and the decode-noise term.
4
Two changes, one read. The arm moved force_bottleneck and sigreg_warmup together. The recon damage cannot be assigned to either from this arm alone.

tc1536, no bottleneck, sigreg 0.005

The plain arm's weight with the decode-noise term added, nothing else. It tests the 12×-sigreg suspect directly and costs one 40 h allocation. If it escapes rank 1.0 like the plain arm does, the weight is the cause and the bottleneck is not needed at all.
