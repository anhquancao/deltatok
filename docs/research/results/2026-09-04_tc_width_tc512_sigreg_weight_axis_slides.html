against the plan's falsifiers · and what is not settled

The bracket closed. The exponent did not.

falsifier 1 — ruled out

"Rank still rising and recon still falling at 0.08"

No. At ep 32 rank is 46.5 against 80.1 at 0.02, and recon is 1.82× worse.

falsifier 2 — not as stated

"Rank peaks and recon turns over at 0.02"

Rank does not peak at 0.02 — it is the lowest of the three plateau arms (93.6 vs 105.1 and 101.9). There is no single turnover point in 0.01–0.04.

falsifier 4 — landed, but at 0.08

"Recon turns over while rank is still climbing"

0.08 has higher rank than 0.002 and 0.005 and far worse recon. The r = −0.99 relation holds inside the healthy set and breaks outside it.

Calibration, as far as it goes. At tc512 the usable band is 0.01–0.04 = 2×–8× the tc128 anchor of 0.005, for a 4× change in Cz. Both ∝ Cz and ∝ √Cz sit inside that band. One width, one seed — the exponent is not measured.
What to use. 0.01 for token reconstruction, 0.04 if the decoded geometry is the target. The difference is under 3% pooled; do not spend a run choosing between them.
