verdict — question 19 · closed 2026-09-17

SIGReg alone is enough on seed 1. The clip avoids the blow-up but costs recon, so it is not adopted.

1
The pool-24576 recovery replicates. Seed 1 without the clip blows up (train ZRowMeanSquare 7,322 at ep 1, rank 1.0 at ep 1–3) and comes back: KITTI rank 27.2 at ep 4, 190.0 at ep 9. The control did the same, so 2 of 2 runs on this recipe recover.
2
Clip 4 skips the blow-up. Peak train ZRowMeanSquare 3.94, ZTotalVar 5,994 vs 10.6M. From ep 5 the train scale matches clip 0 (1.104 vs 1.111 at ep 9), but eval rank stays under half (89.8 vs 190.0 KITTI, 79.9 vs 207.9 nuScenes).
3
It costs recon, and the gap grows. Ep 9 LossRecon +44% KITTI / +42% nuScenes (0.0717 vs 0.0497). PredVsOrig +47–52% KITTI, +66–74% nuScenes. KITTI LossRecon was +17% at ep 5.
4
Against GT the gap is small. PredVsGT +5–11% KITTI, +4–13% nuScenes, held down by the teacher's own OrigVsGT floor.
