On Waymo the tc512 code lives in 128 of 512 directions, so isotropic noise spent 75% of its energy where there is no signal. The two flows put their error in the same directions at every N. More steps only scale it, to ~100% of the signal energy at N = 20. The decoder's answer to that size is the whole difference.

What is still confounded — the decoders differ in training noise (15% vs 72%) and in recipe

Width or recipe? The ft10 recipe on the tc512 tokenizer, run twice with only the noise changed, separates them.

ft10 was a 10-epoch decoder-only finetune whose only loss was the noised decode. The tc512 detached decoder trained 100 epochs from scratch on a clean decode plus a noised one, encoder co-adapting. Both arms below start from the detached epoch_100.pth with the encoder frozen, so the existing flow iter_100000 reads them without retraining.

Findings

The flows are the same. The decoders differ, and the isotropic ladder could not see it.

1
The tc512 code is 128-dimensional on Waymo. SigShareTop128 = 0.9998 of 512, ZPartRank 92.9 vs 94.2 at tc128. Isotropic σ = 0.82 puts 15% of the signal energy into the used subspace at tc512, 72% at tc128.
2
Shaped noise at the same MSEToken flips the ranking. tc512 detached +16% pointmap / +33% raymap at σ = 0.82; ft10 +7.6% / −8.4%; tc128 source +348% / +984%. The earlier "tc512 decoders are flat" read was an artefact of the probe.
3
The flow error's direction profile is width- and N-invariant. ErrShareTop16 0.13 vs signal 0.25–0.27, top-16 error/signal ≈ 0.5, directions 16–128 above 1. N = 1 → 20 moves the shares by < 0.01.
4
More steps only scale the error, to 0.96 / 1.00 of the signal energy at N = 20. Relative to its own code the tc512 flow is the better one (0.67 vs 0.84 at N = 1).
5
So the step-sweep shape is the decoder's. ft10 trained on noise up to 72% of the signal in its subspace and absorbs the flow's 100%; the tc512 detached decoder trained on 15% and breaks between shaped σ = 0.55 and 0.82.
Open. Whether 15% vs 72% is the width (isotropic τ is wasted at tc512) or the recipe (joint clean + noised vs noise-only finetune). Slide 05 separates them with two decoder-only finetunes on the tc512 tokenizer.
Checks passed. Every shaped pass: MSEToken within 0.1% of σ², ErrShare = SigShare to 4 decimals. All five jobs 1-GPU, ~5 min each.
Not done. No decoded frames. The tc512 clean twin was not run on the shaped ladder. Neither finetune arm on slide 05 exists yet; clean_decode_weight is planned but not implemented.
