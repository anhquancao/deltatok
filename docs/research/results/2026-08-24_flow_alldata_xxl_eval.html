Ep 24 anomaly

All three datasets dip sharply at ep 24 — nuScenes Pointmap hits 8.91 (vs 12.72 at ep 77), Waymo drops to 8.21 (vs 11.10 at ep 77). By ep 25 losses revert to ~16 / ~15. Single-epoch effect; likely a lucky eval batch or a transient basin.

Observations

  • Alldata trails Waymo-only at matched epochs. On Waymo eval at ep 77: Pointmap 11.10 vs 9.60 (+16%), Raymap 8.70 vs 7.03 (+24%). Both trained from random init on the same tokenizer. The extra data dilutes per-dataset gradient signal without compensating via transfer.
  • No OOD baseline. The Waymo-only run does not eval on KITTI/nuScenes, so we cannot tell whether alldata helps zero-shot transfer. The alldata OOD numbers (KITTI Ptmap 18.55, nuScenes 12.72) stand alone.
  • KITTI is hardest — Pointmap 6.3× ceiling, Raymap 10.1×. Its tokenizer ceiling is the smallest (Ptmap 2.94), amplifying the ceiling-relative gap.
  • Gap to ceiling is 2–10× across all datasets. Depth is closest (1.2–1.9×). Pointmap and Raymap are 2.7–10.1×. The Waymo-only run has similarly large ceiling gaps, pointing to the flow model architecture or conditioning as the bottleneck, not data.
  • Not converged. CV 4–12% on Pointmap/Raymap over last 10 epochs. Run stopped at ep 77/100 due to walltime exit. Remaining epochs could narrow the gap but are unlikely to close it.
