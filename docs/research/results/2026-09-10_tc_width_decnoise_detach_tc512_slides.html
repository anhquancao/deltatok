what changed

One line of the graph, nothing else.

  • In-graph (BSC:45529827) — the clean decode itself ran on z + σ·ε, so the encoder was trained through the noise.
  • Detached (BSC:45578805) — the clean decode is untouched; a second decode of z.detach() + σ·ε carries its own loss into the decoder alone.
  • Every other knob is the twin's: tc512, sigreg 0.02, ns1024, pool 8192, compose 1.0, τ 0.8, warmup 2000.
verdict

Yes on cost. The payoff is on slide 5.

  • Both in-graph pathologies are gone. The rank halving and the 2.3× gain the encoder used to buy tolerance do not appear.
  • Clean recon is nearly free. The gap peaked at +4.5% (ep 10) and settled to +1.1%, against the in-graph arm's +32.8% peak that never closed.
  • Rank still does not rise. Identical encoder gradients predict exactly that — the original "noisy channel lifts usable rank" hypothesis stays falsified.

What this closes and what it opens

The recipe is bought. Whether the flow can spend it is the next job.

closed

Where the gradient stops was the whole story.

  • The ep-37 read's mechanism is confirmed by removal. Rank halving and gain inflation were the encoder's response to noise it had to survive; take the noise off the encoder and both vanish.
  • Decoder-side noise training is close to free at tc512 — +1.1% Eval/Loss and +9.7% wall clock, against the in-graph arm's +26% that two extrapolations said would not close in budget.
  • Rank is not a lever here. Third axis to leave the ~90/512 shelf untouched, after cov_weight and the noisy channel.
open

Three things this read does not settle.

  • The flow read is the only test that matters. The 2026-09-04 finetune lifted the flow's best read −17.6% raymap at N=20 and hurt N=1; this tokenizer has to be re-measured the same way before it is adopted.
  • σ is still absolute. "σ must go relative" from the ep-37 read is untested — it was aimed at the encoder's gain response, which detaching already removes, so the case for it is weaker but not dead.
  • The arm stopped at ep 61 of 100. One more 40 h BSC allocation matches the twin's budget; the gap is still narrowing, so the read would only improve.
→
2.4× less degradation at σ = 0.82, for +1.1% clean Eval/Loss and +9.7% wall clock. The in-graph arm paid +26% recon for a flatter-looking ladder that was mostly latent gain; detaching buys the real thing at a price worth paying. What stays unmeasured is the only thing the tolerance is for — whether it lifts the flow at N = 1, where the 2026-09-04 decoder finetune made things worse.
