One-at-a-time ablation off the additive-composition tc128 arm. 101 epochs each, BSC, identical code.

Verdict — keep the baseline recipe. Headline number is the mean of the last 5 evals, ep 95–99 (the last window base has; it lost ep 100 to the SLURM wall). No arm beats uniform · t_shared · linear · alpha=0 on any of the three rollout metrics by more than the window's own spread.
  • t_dist=logitnormal — worse: +4.4% Pointmap, +6.5% Raymap. The only arm that keeps its sign in every window (+2.9%/+3.3% on the 40-eval ep60–99 mean, losing 32/40 head-to-head epochs). This is the one resolved effect in the set.
  • t_shared=false — flat: −0.4% / +0.5%.
  • sampler_scheduler_mode=cosine — flat: +0.7% / +0.7%. It reads −1.3% / −2.8% on the ep60–99 mean, so the sign depends on the window: unresolved, not a win.
  • sampler_alpha=0.5 — +2.6% / +5.2% here, but +0.9% / +1.8% one epoch later and +0.4% / +0.1% on ep60–99. Window noise, not a regression.
Depth is flat everywhere (all five within 1.4%), consistent with it sitting closest to the tokenizer ceiling at ×1.37–1.39.

What it changes: whether the two forecast slots share a timestep. Baseline draws one t per sample and broadcasts it, so the whole clip sits at one noise level. This arm draws an independent t per slot, so frame 3 can be nearly clean while frame 4 is nearly noise.
cosineschedmodel.sampler_scheduler_mode=cosineeval sampler447677180–1000.5912df_ctx3fwd2_tc128mg9s005compose_cosinesched_xxl
What it changes: where the 20 ODE steps land in t. Baseline is a uniform grid (progress = i/N); cosine uses 1 − cos(progress·π/2), which starts slow, so more steps fall near t=0 — the noisy end where the velocity field turns fastest.
alpha05model.sampler_alpha=0.5eval sampler447677190–1000.6130df_ctx3fwd2_tc128mg9s005compose_alpha05_xxl
What it changes: per-frame stagger while sampling. Each view integrates at t = progress · (1 + offset·alpha) with offset running 1→0 across views (occrae/flow_matching.py:214), so the earliest forecast frame runs 50% ahead and finishes denoising first. alpha=0 collapses to one shared schedule.

Train loss is the running window at iter 980 of the epoch, quoted only as an identity check. It is not comparable across logitnorm and tindep: both change the t draw, so they optimise a differently-weighted objective. base, cosinesched and alpha05 train identically — their spread (0.591–0.613) is pure run-to-run RNG.

Two of the four knobs never touch training. t_dist and t_shared are read in flow_noising() (occrae/deltatok_flow_trainer.py:472-480) — training-time. sampler_scheduler_mode and sampler_alpha are read only in the eval sampling block (occrae/deltatok_flow_trainer.py:791-792) — eval-time. cosinesched and alpha05 therefore each burned a full 40 h training run to measure a decode-time setting, and their gap to base is contaminated by training RNG. eval_deltatok_flow_sampler.py already sweeps samplers on a fixed checkpoint and is the correct instrument for both.

6 · Why logit-normal loses

The noising is z_t = t·x + (1−t)·e, so t is the data fraction — t=1 is clean data, t=0 is pure noise. With mu=-0.7, sigma=1.4, t = sigmoid(1.4·randn − 0.7) has median sigmoid(−0.7) = 0.332, i.e. the mass moves toward the noise end, not the data end.

That collides with loss_mode=v, whose v-MSE carries a 1/(1−t)² weight and so puts its emphasis at t→1. Logit-normal spends its draws where that weight is smallest. The training loss duly drops (0.499 vs 0.593 at ep90) while every rollout metric gets worse — a textbook case of the train scalar moving opposite to the thing being measured.

Stale comment. All four ablation scripts carry # ABLATED: logit-N(-0.7,1.4); more mass near t=1 in slurm/deltatok_flow/..._compose_logitnorm_bsc.slurm:61. Given z_t = t·x + (1−t)·e the mass is near t=0. The comment inverts the direction; the experiment itself is unaffected.

7 · Distance to the tokenizer ceiling

Base, ep 90–99 mean, as a multiple of the GT-delta rollout on the same batches:

  • Depth 3.842 vs 2.798 → ×1.37. The flow adds 37% on top of the tokenizer. Nearly saturated, and it is why every arm ties on Depth: there is almost nothing left for a t-schedule to move.
  • Pointmap 8.716 vs 4.055 → ×2.15.
  • Raymap 5.928 vs 1.679 → ×3.53. The largest headroom by far, and the metric where the arms spread most — consistent with pose/ray prediction being the part of the forecast the flow is worst at.

None of these ratios shifts by more than 0.12 across the five arms. The sampler and the t schedule are not the binding constraint; the gap to the ceiling is.

9 · Method

  • Source. [Eval/128 @ WaymoSeqMultiView] (Epoch N) lines in the cached stdout under ../monitor_jobs/data/logs/BSC/. All five logs are complete from epoch 0, so the 3.0–3.2 GB TB event files were not needed.
  • Coverage. base ep 0–99 (job 44680758 hit the 40 h wall mid-epoch 100); the four ablations ep 0–100. Every comparison window ends at ep 99 so all five are matched.
  • Code drift. None. The last commit touching occrae/deltatok_flow_trainer.py, occrae/flow_matching.py or occrae/network/ is 5f772d1 (13/08), before base started (16/08 21:52 UTC) and before the ablations (18/08 12:20 UTC).
  • Single eval loader. WaymoSeqMultiView, one [Eval/128] block per epoch. No second held-out set on these arms.

10 · Decision

Keep the baseline. No config change. 
model.t_dist=uniform
model.t_shared=true
model.sampler_scheduler_mode=linear
model.sampler_alpha=0
  • Drop logitnormal permanently. The only resolved effect in the set and it is negative in every window (§4, §3). §6 gives the mechanism, so this is not a noise call.
  • t_shared=true stays. Flat at −0.4% / +0.5%, and it is the matched training prior for sampler_alpha=0 — all slots share one t at sample time, so they should share one at train time.
  • linear stays for the same argument: a uniform-in-t grid is the sampler that matches a uniform-t prior. cosine flips sign between windows (+0.7% vs −1.3% Pointmap), so there is nothing to act on.
  • sampler_alpha=0 stays. 0.5 reads +2.6% / +5.2% on ep95–99 but +0.4% / +0.1% on ep60–99 — window noise, not a measured cost, and not a reason to add stagger either.

Cheap follow-up, if the sampler is worth another look. sampler_scheduler_mode and sampler_alpha never enter training (occrae/deltatok_flow_trainer.py:791-792). Sweeping them on the final base checkpoint with eval_deltatok_flow_sampler.py removes the training RNG entirely and costs minutes. That is the only way these two get resolved rather than re-measured.

Where the headroom is not. §7: the flow sits ×2.15 Pointmap and ×3.51 Raymap above the tokenizer ceiling, and no arm here moved that ratio by more than 0.23. The compose operator, quadruple window and batch-size items in ../plan/2026-08-18_cross_backlog.html are the better spend.

11 · What this does not answer

  • No seed replicates. The noise floor above is within-run epoch jitter, which understates seed-to-seed spread. A 2-seed repeat of base would price the real floor for every future arm at 40 h.
  • cosinesched and alpha05 are unresolved, not refuted. Sweeping them with eval_deltatok_flow_sampler.py on one fixed base checkpoint removes the training RNG entirely and costs minutes, not 40 h.
  • Interactions untested. One-at-a-time only. t_shared=false pairs naturally with sampler_alpha>0 (both stagger t per slot); that combination was not run.
  • Only ~3% of the ceiling gap is in scope. Section 7 says the flow sits 1.4–3.5× above the tokenizer. Nothing in this ablation set addresses that.
