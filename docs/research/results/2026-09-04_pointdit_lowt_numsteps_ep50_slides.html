Trap 1 · LossFlow

5.75 vs 1.00 is not a 5.7× quality gap

Both arms were evaluated at t_dist=uniform. Under the loss_mode=v weight 1/clamp(1−t,0.05)², uniform puts 51.2% of its mass above t=0.95 — a band pointditT sends 0.03% of its training mass to. The column scores how far the eval prior is from the training prior, not how good the samples are. Rank on the decoded metrics only.

One thing it does confirm. LossFlow is now byte-constant across all five passes within each arm (1.0021 and 5.7514). The prior sweep's per-pass scatter was the shared-RNG bug; the dedicated _eval_t_gen fixed it.

Reading it — which falsifier fired, and what it does not settle

This is the “everything degrades” branch

The plan's §1 routing: “Everything degrades → reproduces logitnormal(−0.7, 1.4). The collision is loss_mode=v itself. Hand off to 2026-09-02_pointdit_xloss_additive.md.” That is what the data shows, at ep50.

Takeaway

Do not make the low-t schedule the flow default

It costs 8.2% MSEToken and 19.3% LossRaymap at the operating point it was supposed to improve. The predicted trade — win at N=1, lose at N=20 — did not happen; N=20 is the only place it wins, and only on MSEToken.

Interpretation

Concentrating mass at low t is not the same as training the 1-step map

Under loss_mode=v the schedule also strips the high-t supervision that shapes the whole trajectory. The 1-step readout still reads a model that has to place the sample from pure noise, and that map got worse, not better.

Caveats

What this does not establish

  • ep50, not the ep100 read the plan mandates. pointditT is at epoch 58 of 100 (BSC 45344713). Ordering could still change.
  • One seed, one arm pair. No repeat, no other width, no other tokenizer.
  • Two knobs moved together. t_dist and force_zero_t_ratio changed at once by design, so neither is separately attributed.
  • ODE mode only. The damped sampler was not run.
  • No decode visuals. Both sweeps ran eval_num_visualizations=0, so there are no per-frame decodes or L1 error maps in this deck.
  • Steps > 20 untested. The trend is measured over 1–20 only.
