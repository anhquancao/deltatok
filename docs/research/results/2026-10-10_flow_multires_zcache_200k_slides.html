Runs. Control BSC:46503892, trains at 518×266 only; ep 24, 50k at its 48 h wall. Multires BSC:47022776, heights 294/280/266/210/168; ep 24, 50k, not continued. Z-cache BSC:47058660, heights 280/266/210/168; ep 99, 200k in 44.0 h.
Shared. 4 GPUs × bsize 16, tokenizer epoch_70, xxl DiT, ctx2fwd8, constant lr 1e-4, max_iter 200k, 20-step training-log eval.
Z-cache differs. It reads the frozen DA3 + DeltaTok targets from disk and runs DA3 on frame 0 only. No 294 px height; different data order. Not an exact twin of uncached multires.
Caveats. One seed each. Per-epoch Raymap SD is 0.9–1.3 late in training, so compare window means. Only KITTI old is shared with the control. The control never trained at KITTI's 168 px height, so part of every KITTI gap is resolution coverage.
